WHY THIS EXISTS

AI education should not depend on your wallet.

I'm building this as a free learning resource for people who genuinely want to learn AI. The core goal is simple: bring the concepts, practical exercises, engineering patterns and quality practices into one accessible place.

The platform is designed around free and open-source tools wherever practical, while keeping the learning experience structured, honest and useful. The curriculum contains 150 focused lessons: 17 core phases plus 3 optional specialization phases. We deliberately removed repetition and low-value topic sprawl so the core path stays focused on skills that compound into real AI systems.
