THE MISSION

AI learning should be accessible to everyone.

Puneeth AI Engineering is a free educational project for people who genuinely want to learn AI without expensive courses becoming a barrier.

What we're building

A single, structured place where a learner can move from fundamentals to practical AI engineering: concepts, code, projects, testing, evaluation and production thinking.

Our principles

  • Free core learning. The essential curriculum should remain accessible without a paywall.
  • Step-by-step progression. Learners build prerequisites instead of being thrown into advanced topics too early.
  • Practice over memorization. Each concept should lead toward something the learner can build and verify.
  • Quality matters. AI systems should be tested and evaluated, not trusted simply because they produce an answer.
  • Responsible use. AI should be used thoughtfully, with attention to security, privacy, safety and real-world impact.

About the creator

I'm Puneeth, and I'm building this as a long-term open learning resource. The goal isn't to sell a course. The goal is to make a useful path available to anyone who wants to learn.

Core learning is designed to remain free. Where infrastructure choices are needed, we will prioritize free tiers, open-source tools and locally runnable options wherever practical.
