THE CREATOR

Built by Puneeth.

A practical, engineering-first curriculum for people who want to understand AI systems rather than only use them.

The philosophy

Start with fundamentals. Build small things yourself. Compare the from-scratch implementation with the framework abstraction. Test the system. Then ship it.

What makes this different

  • Concepts connected into a deliberate progression.
  • Runnable examples rather than passive notes.
  • AI engineering and AI QE treated as engineering disciplines.
  • Production concerns—evaluation, observability, security and reliability—are part of the learning path.

Open by design

The curriculum is designed to live in Git, evolve continuously, and remain useful as the AI ecosystem changes.
