# Curriculum Final Decision — 150 Lessons

## Final shape

- **20 phases total**
- **17 core phases**
- **3 optional specialization phases**: Computer Vision (05), LLM Internals & Training (09), Multimodal AI (12)
- **150 lessons total**
- Optional phases never block the core progression.

01 Python for AI

04 Deep Learning with PyTorch

07 Transformers
08 LLM Foundations & Inference
10 LLM Application Engineering
11 RAG Engineering
13 Tools & Protocols

17 Production & LLMOps
18 Security & Responsible AI
19 Capstone & Portfolio

## Optional branches

05 Computer Vision
09 LLM Internals & Training
12 Multimodal AI

## Why 150?

150 is not a target to celebrate. It is a consequence of cutting duplication and moving specializations out of the mandatory path. The number can change again if editorial review finds a lesson that should be merged or split.
